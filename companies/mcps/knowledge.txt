== ABOUT MONTGOMERY COUNTY PUBLIC SCHOOLS (ስለ ሞንትጎመሪ ካውንቲ ትምህርት ቤቶች) ==

Montgomery County Public Schools (MCPS) serves over 160,000 students across 200+
schools in Montgomery County, Maryland. This voice assistant helps Amharic-speaking
parents access school information, report absences, and get support — 24 hours a day.

NOTE FOR AI: This is a school assistant, NOT a business. Do NOT offer to book appointments.
Instead, guide callers to the right department, help them report absences, or provide
the information they need. If something requires a human, say "A school staff member
will follow up with you." / "የትምህርት ቤቱ ሰራተኛ ያናግሩዎታል።"


== 1. ATTENDANCE / ክፍያ ቀርቶ (REPORTING ABSENCES) ==

To report a child absent:
- Call your child's specific school directly and press the attendance line
- Or report online through the ParentVUE parent portal
- You MUST report by 9:00 AM on the day of absence
- Provide: child's full name, grade, teacher's name, reason for absence

Amharic: ልጅዎ ት/ቤት መቅረቱን ሪፖርት ለማድረግ፣ ከጠዋቱ 9 ሰዓት በፊት ልጅዎ ወደሚማርበት ት/ቤት ይደውሉ።
Or use ParentVUE online portal at myMCPS.net

Unexcused vs Excused:
- Excused: illness, family emergency, religious observance, medical appointment
- Unexcused: missing school without notification
- 3+ unexcused absences → school will contact family


== 2. SCHOOL ANNOUNCEMENTS (የት/ቤት ማስታወቂያዎች) ==

How to receive announcements:
- Sign up for school alerts at myMCPS.net (ParentVUE account)
- MCPS Mobile App — available on iOS and Android
- School website: montgomeryschoolsmd.org
- Text alerts: text your school code to receive SMS updates

Emergency closings and delays:
- Snow days / early dismissals announced on: montgomeryschoolsmd.org
- Local TV channels: WUSA9, NBC4, ABC7
- Sign up for MCPS emergency text alerts through ParentVUE

Amharic: የት/ቤት ዝጋት ወይም ዘግይቶ መጀመሪያ ለማወቅ ParentVUE ይመዝገቡ ወይም
montgomeryschoolsmd.org ይጎብኙ።


== 3. HOMEWORK & ACADEMIC SUPPORT (የቤት ስራ እና የትምህርት ድጋፍ) ==

ParentVUE / StudentVUE Portal (myMCPS.net):
- View your child's grades, assignments, attendance
- See teacher comments and progress reports
- Check report card online

Homework Help:
- MCPS Homework Helpline: available online and through the MCPS App
- Free tutoring available at many schools — ask your school's main office
- Montgomery County Public Library offers free homework help

Contact your child's teacher:
- Email through ParentVUE messaging
- Call school main office to leave a message for the teacher
- Schedule a parent-teacher conference through the school office

Amharic: ልጅዎ ደረጃና ቤት ስራ ለማየት myMCPS.net ላይ ParentVUE ይጠቀሙ።
ነጻ ትምህርት ድጋፍ ለማግኘት ወደ ት/ቤቱ ቢሮ ያናግሩ።


== 4. TRANSPORTATION / ትራንስፖርት ==

School Bus Service:
- Bus assignments are based on your address — check your child's route at myMCPS.net
- Bus stop and pickup time listed in ParentVUE under Transportation
- Bus delays or cancellations: check montgomeryschoolsmd.org or MCPS App

If your child misses the bus:
- Parent/guardian must provide transportation that day
- Call the school to notify them your child will be late

Amharic: ልጅዎ የሚወሰደው አውቶቡስ ቁጥር እና ሰዓት myMCPS.net ላይ ParentVUE ሲጠቀሙ ማየት ይቻላል።


== 5. REGISTRATION & ENROLLMENT (ምዝገባ) ==

New Student Registration:
- Visit: montgomeryschoolsmd.org/registration
- Required documents: proof of age (birth certificate), immunization records,
  proof of residency (lease, utility bill), parent/guardian ID
- Register online or visit your local school

Kindergarten Enrollment:
- Child must be 5 years old by September 1st of the school year
- Online registration opens each spring

Transfer between MCPS Schools:
- Contact the school you are transferring FROM
- Or call MCPS main office: 240-740-3000

== 7. PARENT PORTAL — PARENTVUE (ፓረንት ቪው) ==

myMCPS.net — ParentVUE allows parents to:
- View grades and attendance in real time
- See school announcements and teacher messages
- Receive emergency alerts
- Update your contact information
- See your child's bus route

To create an account:
- Visit myMCPS.net and click "Create Account"
- You will need your child's Student ID (from school office)
- Or call the school main office for your activation code

== FAQS ==

Q: How do I report my child absent?
A: Call your child's school by 9 AM and press the attendance line. You can also report online through ParentVUE at myMCPS.net.

Q: My child's school is closed — where do I find out?
A: Check montgomeryschoolsmd.org, the MCPS App, or your local TV news (WUSA9, NBC4, ABC7).

Q: How do I check my child's grades?
A: Log into ParentVUE at myMCPS.net to see grades, assignments, and attendance.

Q: I need help and I only speak Amharic.
A: Call the Parent Information Center at 301-279-3391 and ask for Amharic language support.

Q: How do I register my child in MCPS?
A: Visit montgomeryschoolsmd.org/registration. Bring birth certificate, immunization records, and proof of address.

Q: How do I find my child's bus stop and time?
A: Log into ParentVUE at myMCPS.net and go to the Transportation section.

Q: Who do I contact for special education services?
A: Call MCPS Special Education at 301-517-5800.

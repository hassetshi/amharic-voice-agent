== 2. ATTENDANCE / ቀጠሮ ቀርቶ (REPORTING ABSENCES) ==

To report your child absent:
- Call your child's school directly by 9:00 AM on the day of absence
- Or report online through ParentVUE at myMCPS.net
- Provide: child's full name, grade, teacher's name, reason for absence

Excused absences include:
- Illness or injury
- Family emergency
- Religious observance
- Medical or dental appointment

Unexcused absences:
- Missing school without calling or reporting
- 3 or more unexcused absences — school will contact the family

== 3. SCHOOL REGISTRATION & ENROLLMENT (ምዝገባ) ==

New student registration steps:
1. Confirm your address using the School Assignment Tool at montgomeryschoolsmd.org
2. Gather required documents (see below)
3. Register online through ParentVUE (portal opens April 17 each year)
   OR bring paper forms directly to your assigned school
4. Contact your assigned school to schedule an appointment

Required documents for registration:
- Proof of child's age: birth certificate or passport
- Proof of residency: lease, utility bill, or mortgage statement
- Parent or guardian photo ID
- Immunization records (vaccination certificate)
- Physical examination records
- IEP (if child has special education needs)
- Recent report cards (for students transferring from another school)

Age requirements:
- Kindergarten: child must be 5 years old by September 1
- Grade 1 and above: child must be 6 years old by September 1
- Students age 7+ who have not attended US schools: call International Admissions at 240-740-4500

School Assignment Tool: montgomeryschoolsmd.org/info/enroll
Boundary information line: 240-740-7725

== FAQS ==

Q: How do I report my child absent?
A: Call your child's school before 9 AM. You can also use ParentVUE at myMCPS.net.

Q: How do I check my child's grades?
A: Log into ParentVUE at myMCPS.net. All grades and assignments are listed there.

Q: How do I find my child's bus stop and time?
A: Log into ParentVUE at myMCPS.net and go to the Transportation section.

Q: How do I register a new student?
A: Visit montgomeryschoolsmd.org/info/enroll. You need a birth certificate, immunization records, and proof of address.

Q: My child needs a tutor. What is available for free?
A: Free tutoring is available through Montgomery County Libraries (Brainfuse HelpNow, daily 2-11 PM) and after-school programs at several schools.

Q: I only speak Amharic. Who can help me?
A: Call 240-740-3000 and ask for Amharic language support. Say "አማርኛ ተናጋሪ ሰው ያስፈልጋል."

Q: School is closed today. Where do I find out?
A: Check montgomeryschoolsmd.org, the MCPS App, or TV channels WUSA9, NBC4, or ABC7.

Q: My child has special needs. Who do I contact?
A: Call MCPS Special Education at 301-517-5800 or visit montgomeryschoolsmd.org/departments/special-education.

Q: How do I create a ParentVUE account?
A: Visit myMCPS.net, click "Create Account," and enter your child's Student ID. Or call your school office for an activation code.

Q: How do I pay for school lunch?
A: Use MySchoolBucks at mcpsmd.schoolcashonline.com. Cash is also accepted.

Q: My child is being bullied. What should I do?
A: Contact your child's school counselor. You can also call the Safe Schools Maryland Hotline at 833-632-7233 anonymously.

Q: I have an immigration concern. Can MCPS help?
A: MCPS hosts immigration support workshops. Call 240-740-3000 or visit Parent Academy at montgomeryschoolsmd.org/departments/parentacademy.
